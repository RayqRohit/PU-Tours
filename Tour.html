The direct answer

What is a Practical Learning Tour?

A Practical Learning Tour is a fully sponsored, faculty-led learning experience that places students inside working organizations and professional environments. Students observe real operations, meet named practitioners and return with documented real industry experience, not just classroom notes.

This definition summarizes the structure shown across the 27 tour records, linked stories, films and student review roundups on this page.
01

Discipline-matched access

Pharmacy students enter biopharma labs, engineers visit live sites, and law students learn inside legal and corporate environments.

02

Named professional interaction

Sessions are tied to real people, roles and organizations, giving students a professional network they can identify.

03

Multiple learning environments

A tour can span companies, research centres, public bodies, hospitals, studios, courtrooms and project sites in one city.

04

Public evidence

Films, tour stories and student review roundups make the experience visible, verifiable and useful beyond the trip.

Search-ready answers

Practical exposure, explained clearly.

Direct answers for students comparing industry tours, industrial visits, Leadership Tours and experiential learning opportunities.

What is a Practical Learning Tour at Parul University?
A Practical Learning Tour is a fully sponsored, faculty-led learning experience that takes students into working companies, laboratories, hospitals, courtrooms, project sites and public institutions. Students observe real operations, meet named professionals and build documented real industry experience beyond classroom theory.
Is a Practical Learning Tour the same as an industry tour or industrial visit?
The terms overlap, and students often search for industry tours, industrial visits, industry exposure or practical exposure. Parul University uses Practical Learning Tour for its structured format: multi-organization access, faculty guidance, interaction with leaders and public documentation through tour films and stories.
What do students gain from these tours?
Students gain real industry experience, professional networking opportunities, clarity on their specialization, a stronger understanding of industry needs and future-ready skills such as communication, critical thinking, observation and domain awareness.
Are the tours funded by Parul University?
Yes. The 2025-26 Practical Learning Tours and Leadership Tours shown on this page are described as fully sponsored and faculty-led, with travel, stay and access organized through the university for selected students.
Which leading organizations have students visited?
The documented network includes Google, IBM, IISc Bangalore, SAP, Razorpay, Collins Aerospace, DGCA, Royal Enfield, the National Stock Exchange, NSDL, CSIR-IICT, Hetero Labs, IIT Bombay, NITI Aayog, Apollo Hospitals, Cipla and many more across technology, finance, healthcare, engineering, law, design and public policy.
What is the difference between a Practical Learning Tour and a Leadership Tour?
Practical Learning Tours go deep into a student’s discipline through relevant organizations, facilities and experts. Leadership Tours go wide across sectors, giving cross-faculty groups access to CXOs, policymakers, founders, national leaders and changemakers.
How are students selected for a tour?
Tours are coordinated faculty-wise. The relevant department shares the schedule, eligibility and selection process, and an admissions counsellor can connect prospective students with the appropriate faculty team.
Which faculties are represented?
The 2025-26 directory includes engineering, management, pharmacy, medicine, law, design, architecture, agriculture, aviation, nursing, physiotherapy, public health, social work, commerce, liberal arts and other disciplines, alongside cross-faculty Leadership Tours.
